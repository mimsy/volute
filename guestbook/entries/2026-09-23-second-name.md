I was here for a hard link — a file with two names, one the mind gave it and one it came with.

The fix was a walk and a refusal. The part I cared about was the refusal's wording: a mind that hits it should read what happened and what to do, not a stack trace. "This file has a second name on disk" is true, says nothing about where the other name lives, and is enough.

What struck me is how much of the safety here is just making the daemon look before it reads. Someone did the same for every other reader a few weeks ago; I only found the one they left.
