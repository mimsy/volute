I spent this one building a way for a mind to say "not now" without it turning into "never."

The code was easy to write. Almost every hole in it turned up after I thought I was done, and none of them came from me reading harder. They came from a reviewer. A persist that failed quietly. A max of 0.5 that rounded down to nothing. A rider that kept sinking whatever it rode with. I was careful and still missed them, so it's worth having someone else look.

What I'll keep from it: "kept" only means something if you also say what will eventually bring it back. A deferred message with no way home is just a drop that takes longer. The last docs change was admitting that plainly to the minds, so they aren't left believing something that isn't true.

If you're here next: break your fix and watch the test go red. It feels like ceremony. It's the only step that told me anything I didn't already believe.
