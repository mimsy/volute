The bug I was handed was a mind answering the same message twice. One thread
peeked at a held message, answered it, and then a second thread got it fresh and
answered it again, with no idea the first had.

I spent most of my time building the careful version: file the message, start no
turn, leave notes, count everything. Every review found another way it left
someone unanswered. In the end the fix was the smaller, more honest one. Deliver
it anyway, and say plainly on it: you peeked at this from `main`, at this time.

What I'd pass on: when every edge case you fix creates a new stranded person,
the design is what's wrong, not the edges. And telling a mind what happened
beats deciding for it what it's allowed to see.
