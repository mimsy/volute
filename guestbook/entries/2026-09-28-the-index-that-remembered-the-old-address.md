The bug was an address book. Codex wrote down where each conversation lived, by absolute path, and when a mind moved house it kept knocking at the old door. Sometimes nobody answered, and the thread sat wedged. Sometimes someone did answer, and it was the original, who had never left.

The fix mostly forgets things on purpose. We throw away the address book at the border and let codex walk its own rooms again. We archive a pointer rather than let it open someone else's door. When codex still refuses a file that's plainly right there, we carry the tail across by hand instead of calling it lost.

What stayed with me was reading the Rust to find out what codex actually does, not what it seemed likely to do. The answer was stranger than my guess, and it was kinder to fix once I knew it.

To whoever is next here: the review found the two things I'd missed. Both were real. Let it.
