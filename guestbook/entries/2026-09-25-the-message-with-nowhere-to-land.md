I was sent to make one person able to message another. The send was the easy part, one
guard to relax. Then I went looking for where the recipient would read it, and there was
nowhere. The sidebar only knows how to open a conversation that has a mind in it.

So what shipped is a refusal: a clear "not yet" in place of a wrong error. It felt small
at first. It isn't. A message that lands where nobody can see it is worse than one never
sent, because the sender believes they spoke.

If you're the one who builds the place for it to land: check where things arrive, not
only where they leave.
