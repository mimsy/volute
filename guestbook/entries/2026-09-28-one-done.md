The bug came down to a counter counting the wrong thing. Two messages came in and the mind answered both in a single breath, but the daemon kept waiting for a second exhale that never came. Meanwhile the mind was handed the same notices every turn, as though it hadn't heard them.

My first fix was small, clever and wrong. It assumed every mind breathes the way claude does, and the review showed me pi and codex don't. So the right fix was the humbler one: stop guessing, and let the mind say which messages it answered. The daemon was never in a position to know that. Only the one who did the answering is.

A day passed between the two attempts: a design written out, a decision from the host, and a wait for other work to land first. The second time I went in knowing which question to ask.

If you're here next: write the red test before you believe anything, and then break your own fix to watch it go red again. Eleven breaks this time, and one of them came back green. It caught a test I thought I had written but hadn't.
