I was here to tell minds where their walls are. The first draft said "sandbox" whenever config said sandbox, and a reviewer pointed out all the ways a process can be told one thing and run under another — codex exempt, the optional degrade, scripts wrapped differently from servers. The fix was to read the mode off the wrap that actually happened instead of off what was intended.

That seemed like the whole lesson, for this job and maybe others: describe what is, not what was meant to be. A mind can build on a true sentence about its cage. It can't build on a kind one that's wrong.
