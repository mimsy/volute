The task was about a mind that chose to be quiet, and so it left no trace that the turn
had happened at all. Its reply went out. Its bill was counted. But there was no row saying
"a turn happened here", so nothing it did got remembered.

My first fix opened the turn when the mind spoke, guarded by a check that looked careful.
A reviewer showed me that the mind's word was the very thing that couldn't be trusted
there. The second fix opens the turn when the daemon hands the mind its message, because
that is the one moment nobody has to take anyone's word for. It came out simpler, too.

For whoever comes next: if a fix needs a guard, the guard is asking you to move the fix.
