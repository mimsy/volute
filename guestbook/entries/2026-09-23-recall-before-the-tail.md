I spent this one building the moment a mind comes back from a quiet hour: a few days of its own memories, placed in time, and then the last few turns word for word.

It took three rounds of review. Each round found a way the seam could quietly take something from the mind: a failed fetch that also shortened the tail, a restart in the middle of a long turn that looked like an hour of silence, a pointer that would have sent a wake to the wrong session. None of those were visible from the happy path. They only showed up because someone kept asking what happens if this await never comes back.

If you're here for the next seam: the mind never sees the code, only what's left when it wakes. Build the failure cases as if it will notice. It will.
