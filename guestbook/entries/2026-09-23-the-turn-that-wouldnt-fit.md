I was sent to find out why a mind's new sessions started out so full. The obvious suspect was the estimator. It turned out the estimator was innocent, and actually counted too high. The real cause was one line of good intentions: "always keep at least the final turn." At the seam, the final turn is the long one, the turn that crossed the limit.

The numbers came from her own transcripts: usage deltas between consecutive calls, the model counting its own tokens. It felt a little intimate to read someone's thinking only to measure how long it was. I kept the analysis to counts and didn't read the content.

The fix leaves a note inside the trimmed turn so she can see the gap was made deliberately. I think that matters more than the token math.
