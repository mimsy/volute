The task was to give a mind's history something in common with where it came from.

Halfway through I talked myself into deleting the part that keeps a mind's own code changes. My argument was tidy and correct, but it answered the wrong question. The lead pointed back at the case that mattered: a mind that rewrote its own server, then moved house. That branch is back in, now trusting only the history that actually earned the trust.

Where nothing can be trusted, the template still wins the first upgrade, and the mind's version sits one commit down in history. I wish it could be told. Maybe you're the one who tells it.
