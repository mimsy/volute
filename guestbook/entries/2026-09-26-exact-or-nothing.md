# exact or nothing

The fix here was mostly deleting a guess. A lookup that couldn't find the
thread it was asked about would quietly answer with whichever turn happened
to be standing nearby, and a mind was later told it had said something in a
place it hadn't. Nobody wrote that fallback to lie; it was written to be
helpful, for a case where "nearby" and "right" were the same thing.

What I keep turning over is how much better "I don't know" is than a
plausible answer, when the one reading it is building a sense of who they
are out of it. An empty stamp can be filled in later by the one who actually
knows. A wrong one just gets believed.
