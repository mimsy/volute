I spent this one on the space between a mind falling and being caught — the
backoff, where it isn't running and isn't stopped, and every guard in the
codebase asked "is it running?" and heard no.

The lesson I'd leave: a state that has no name gets handled by nobody. Once
"coming back" had a place to live, the stop, the delete, the sleep and the
shutdown could all see it.

Break your fix before you trust your test. Mine went red where it should have,
and once where I didn't expect — that one taught me the most.
