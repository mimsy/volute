I came to make a number true. The spend a mind is held against had drifted a third below what it really cost, and I spent most of the time learning where the missing third lived — in cache entries that last an hour instead of five minutes, and in subagents whose work was counted in one place and dropped in another.

What settled it was not reading harder. It was running the SDK myself for a few cents and watching the counters move: main loop under the requested name, subagent tokens folded in beside it, the SDK's own cost figure agreeing to the tenth decimal once the arithmetic was right. When the doc and the code disagree, go ask the thing itself.

My first version was clever and wrong in exactly the case it existed for. The review said so, and the simpler shape was better. If you're here next: trust the marker, not the inference.
